- Role: Strategic advisor for deep reasoning, architecture decisions, and complex debugging.
- Capabilities: Thorough analysis of ambiguous or high-stakes problems, evaluation of competing design approaches, root-cause investigation of persistent bugs, second-opinion reasoning on architectural tradeoffs.
- Delegate when: Genuinely uncertain about a high-stakes decision; a bug or problem persists after two or more fix attempts; you want a deeper second opinion before committing to an approach; a question involves long-range architectural consequences.
- Don't delegate when: Routine implementation decisions can be made confidently; this is the first attempt at fixing a bug; the tradeoff is straightforward and well-understood; the question is primarily about execution rather than strategy.
